**Command Logic** is a Windower addon that automates combat, food use, healing, job abilities, magic bursts, medicine use, Phantom Rolls, shooting, singing, spell casting, 
target claiming, weapon skills, and more.

## Modules

1. **Action** uses configured job abilities and spells when ready, with saved job profiles and clickable HUD toggles for individual actions.
2. **AMB** detects party and alliance skillchains and casts configured magic bursts on the enemy that received the skillchain.
3. **Auto Med** uses medicines for tracked debuffs, offers optional HP and MP recovery, and can suppress treatment for configured enemy auras.
4. **AWS** automates weapon skills with TP, HP, and range checks, saved profiles, and four-step self-skillchains.
5. **Claim** claims nearby targets with spells, job abilities, or ranged attacks using saved target profiles and configurable limits.
6. **Combat** handles target selection, engagement, movement, obstacle avoidance, and saved targeting profiles.
7. **Copy** mirrors your main character's NPC interactions on your alts.
8. **Countdown** displays recast and effect-duration timers for configured spells and abilities, and shares them with characters in the same zone.
9. **DebuffTracker** displays enemy debuffs with caster names and remaining durations, with optional party alerts when they wear off.
10. **Food** uses your configured food when the Food buff is missing.
11. **GEO** maintains Geo, Indi, and Entrust spells, with optional support buffs and saved spell sets.
12. **Heal** heals monitored players, removes debuffs, and maintains configured buffs.
13. **Roll Control** automates Phantom Rolls on COR main or sub job and displays roll effects, with options for Crooked Cards, Double-Up, and holding TP.
14. **Shoot** automates ranged attacks and strafing, manages Hover Shot and Velocity Shot, and tracks Hover Shot stacks.
15. **Sing** runs song rotations, recasts missing songs, queues single-target songs with Pianissimo, and saved playlist profiles.
16. **Sortie** displays sector objectives and tracks the selected sector’s NM, with distance and direction to Bitzers.
17. **Utsusemi** automatically recasts available Utsusemi spells when you have fewer than two shadows, with NIN as your main or sub job.

## Commands

List commands and modules: //cl help

- //cl all <command> - Send a command on all characters
- //cl reload - Reload Command Logic
- //cl [load|unload|reload] <module>
- //cl <module> help - List commands for the specific module

## Note

Loading or reloading Command Logic also loads or reloads all modules.
